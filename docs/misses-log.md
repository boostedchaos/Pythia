# PYTHIA misses log

The one measurement PYTHIA cannot collect about itself (plan §6): anything significant in your
five beats that the daily brief FAILED to surface. Misses are the number that matters most.

**How to use:** the moment you learn something important from any other channel — a news app,
a colleague, a feed, a paper — that the brief should have told you first, add one line here.
Takes 30 seconds. Honesty beats neatness.

**Target:** ≤ 2 misses per week (plan §6). Reviewed when Phase 2 is built, so every miss here
directly tunes what the ranking learns to surface.
